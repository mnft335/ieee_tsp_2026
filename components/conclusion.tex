In this paper, we characterized proxy-graph inaccuracies in graph signal recovery through two failure modes, excessive penalties and information loss, and proposed a magnitude-adaptive regularizer derived via infimal convolution and equivalent to a scaled separable Huber penalty to mitigate the former.
The proposed regularizer retains GLR-like quadratic evaluation for ordinary proxy-weighted edge differences while limiting the influence of excessively large ones through linear growth, and the resulting constrained convex problem can be efficiently solved by the proposed P-PDS-based algorithm.
Synthetic and real-world experiments supported the effectiveness of the proposed method under controlled and naturally imperfect proxy graphs.
Developing regularization that can additionally recover graph information lost through missing edges or underestimated weights remains an important direction for future work.
